ROOTED IN COMMUNITY

Bringing premium coffee culture to Soweto.

Bean Charge is a premium mobile coffee experience proudly based in Soweto, South Africa. Since 2019, we've served specialty-grade coffee at weddings, street markets, private events, festivals, brand activations, and corporate functions throughout Gauteng.

With over 500 events catered, our mission has remained simple — create memorable coffee moments while bringing people together through exceptional service, authentic hospitality, and expertly brewed espresso.

Our professional mobile setup includes a commercial Brasilia espresso machine, freshly ground beans, handcrafted beverages, and a warm atmosphere inspired by modern café culture and the spirit of Soweto.
