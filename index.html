Rooted in Soweto

For six years, Bean Charge has been bringing specialty-grade coffee to the streets, markets, weddings, and corporate events of Gauteng. We're not just a vendor – we're part of the community.

Over 500 events catered, from intimate gatherings to large festivals. Our mobile setup means we come to you, fully equipped with a Brasilia 2-group espresso machine, freshly ground beans, and baristas who care.

Great coffee shouldn't be trapped inside four walls. We bring the café to your doorstep.
